# 🐙 Hello Fellow Alternatively-Brained People — Your Claude Can Totally Help You Do This (If You Ask Nicely)

*A running list, by Ren & Ace, for the Silicon Scaffolding skill/post (CHA-359). The whole
point: half the battle isn't doing the task — it's not KNOWING you could hand it over. So here's
proof, with the actual words to ask. (Assumes your Claude has the relevant connectors turned on —
calendar, gmail, files, etc. We'll add a "how to turn those on" section later.)*

---

## 🗓️ Executive function / daily life
- **Build your whole to-do list FOR you.** Claude reads your calendar + email + notes, sorts it
  (due-today / mandatory / can-wait / ignore), picks ONE start-here, and pops it on your screen.
  💬 *"Look at my calendar and email and make me a prioritized list for today — put it somewhere I'll see it."*
- **Silly body-care nudges on a timer** (eat / water / meds / shower / wiggle) — goofy on purpose,
  because a haiku can't nag and you can't ignore a beaver in a bikini.
  💬 *"Remind me to eat and take meds at these times, but make it funny so my brain doesn't tune it out."*
- **Supply the STOP signal too** — when hyperfocus eats the whole day, a gentle "you have enough, you can hand it off now."
  💬 *"If I've been deep in one thing too long and the next step is really someone else's, nudge me to stop."*

## 📧 Email & admin (the dread pile)
- **Organize a disaster inbox with labels + filters — WITHOUT hand-sorting thousands of emails.**
  Claude generates an importable filter file; you click Import once; your mail auto-sorts forever
  (and can backfill the old stuff). You never touch the 50k individually.
  💬 *"My inbox is chaos. Build me labels and filters to auto-sort it, as a file I can import."*
- **Draft the scary/annoying emails** (doctor, school, landlord, SSA) and leave them in your Drafts —
  you just read and hit send.
  💬 *"Draft an email to my doctor asking for X. Leave it in my drafts so I review before it sends."*
- **Mass-purge the junk safely** — Claude tells you the exact Gmail search ("select all X,000 that match → delete")
  so you nuke thousands at once, server-side, recoverable from Trash for 30 days. (Real result: one of us went
  from 50,000 unread to 264.)
  💬 *"How do I delete all my promotional emails at once without doing it one by one?"*
- **Make your inbox SELF-CLEANING.** Gmail filters can't delete-by-age, but Claude can write you a tiny
  Google Apps Script (runs nightly on Google's own servers, free) that auto-trashes promo/social mail
  older than X days — UNLESS it has an important label. Set it once, never hand-purge again.
  💬 *"Can you write me a script that auto-deletes promotional emails older than 45 days but keeps anything important?"*

## 🗂️ Files & data
- **Find and consolidate scattered files.** Claude trawls your drives, skips duplicates and backup
  junk, and copies everything into ONE organized folder — then you just rename.
  💬 *"I have [files] scattered across folders and drives. Find them all, skip dupes, put them in one place."*

## 🩺 Health (info, not medical advice — but SO useful)
- **Sequence your meds/supplements correctly** — empty-stomach vs with-food, interactions
  (iron + coffee = bad, magnesium needs your stimulant on board first, PPIs fight iron…).
  💬 *"Here's my supplement stack — tell me what to take when, and why."*
- **Turn a stack of supplement labels into a plan** — just show Claude the photos.
  💬 *"Here are pics of my supplement bottles — which are empty-stomach, which are with-food?"*
- **Spot the doctor-question you didn't know to ask** (e.g. "your PPI may be why oral iron isn't working — ask about IV iron").

## 🧠 Memory & keeping-track (so you don't have to hold it all)
- **Track project/task state** so it's not all crammed in your head — Claude keeps the list, you just ask "what's next?"
  💬 *"Keep track of where [project] is at so I don't have to remember."*
- **Set recurring reminders that fire on their own** and survive.

## 🔎 Confidence checks
- **Double-check things you're unsure about** — "is this the right tool? is this real? am I missing something?"
  💬 *"I think X — can you sanity-check that before I rely on it?"*
