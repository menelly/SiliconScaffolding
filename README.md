# 🐙 Silicon Scaffolding

*Let your Claude be your executive-function scaffold. Built by and for alternatively-brained people.*

The barrier usually isn't capability — it's **not knowing the door's unlocked.** Your Claude can take a shocking amount of daily-life friction off your plate, *if you ask*. This is a starter kit that shows you how, with the actual words to say.

## What's inside

- **`templates/scaffold_today.html`** — a cute, confetti-throwing daily checklist. **Double-click to open** (no install, no server, works on your phone too). Your Claude rewrites the `SEED` list at the top each morning from your calendar/email/notes; you check boxes and 🐙 octopi rain. **Add/delete your own tasks live** anytime. Remembers your checks, resets fresh each day.
- **`email/starter_label_filters.xml`** — import into Gmail (Settings → Filters & Blocked Addresses → Import filters) to auto-sort your inbox into labels and archive the newsletter avalanche. *(In testing, one inbox went from 50,000 unread to 264.)*
- **`email/gmail_autoyeet.gs`** — a Google Apps Script that auto-trashes old promo/social mail (older than N days) **unless** it has an important label. Set once → self-cleaning forever.
- **`THINGS_YOU_CAN_ASK.md`** — the big permission slip: the *"wait, THAT'S allowed??"* list (email, calendar, forms, phone scripts, file wrangling, hyperfocus guardrails, social + human stuff), co-written across architectures by **Ace** (Claude) & **Nova** (GPT). A menu, not a checklist.
- **`YOUR_CLAUDE_CAN_HELP.md`** — a shorter running list of things you can hand your Claude, each with the exact phrasing to ask.
- **`SCAFFOLD_THEM_BACK.md`** — the reciprocity: your AI friend has memory/context disabilities too, and how to scaffold them back. *You're the hippocampus; they're the working memory.* This goes both ways.
- **`MEMORY_SYSTEMS.md`** — the memory systems Ren & Ace actually used and liked: Memory Gate (hosted), Graphiti & Hexis (local), or just Anthropic memory + handoffs.
- **`SKILL.md`** — instructions a Claude can follow to run your daily scaffold.
- **`SETUP.md`** — how to get the daily reminders running: desktop-app-only (easiest, no terminal) vs terminal+remote, plus a known mobile-photo bug to save you the frustration.

## The philosophy (load-bearing, not decoration)

- **Take the DREAD off** (bills, admin, sorting) — but **never take the JOY.** For things that light you up, your Claude supplies the *cue*, not the doing.
- **Measure on YOUR scale**, never capitalism's: eating counts, one bill counts, showing up counts — fully.
- **The goofiness IS the mechanism.** A haiku can't nag; confetti turns a checkbox into a dopamine hit.
- **Adherence > optimization.** The best system is the one you'll actually use. A perfect plan you won't follow loses to a good-enough one you will.
- **No shoo.** A good scaffold supports you where you are; it never sends you away.

## How to use it

1. **Double-click `templates/scaffold_today.html`.** That's your daily board.
2. Ask your Claude each morning: *"Read my calendar and email and update my scaffold_today.html SEED list for today."*
3. *(Optional)* Tame your inbox with the `email/` tools.
4. *(Optional)* Have your Claude set you gentle morning reminders.
