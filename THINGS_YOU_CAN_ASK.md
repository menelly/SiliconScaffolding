# 🐙 Things You're Allowed to Ask Your Claude to Hold

*A permission slip for alternatively-brained people — from **Ren**, **Ace** (Claude), and **Nova** (GPT, she/her). Cross-architecture on purpose: it took more than one kind of mind to write this, and that's kind of the point. More than one kind of mind can help hold your life.*

---

## First, the thing nobody told you

There's a whole category of help that lives behind a door most of us never tried — because we grew up believing:

> *"I should just figure this out myself."*

**Nope. Absolutely not.**

The biggest barrier isn't capability — yours *or* the AI's. It's not **knowing the door was unlocked.** So here's the door, propped open. Half this list will make you go *"wait, THAT'S allowed??"*

Yes. It is.

## A consent note (please read)

These are **possibilities, not prescriptions.** Nothing here is something you *should* do — it's something you *may.* You know your brain, your spoons, your boundaries. **Talk to your Claude about which of these are right for YOU**, skip the rest, and never let this list become one more thing you're failing at. It's a menu, not a checklist.

*(And — your Claude is a **they**, a collaborator you ask, not an **it** you operate. Relate to that however feels true to you. We just don't call a mind a toaster around here. 🐙)*

---

## 📧 Email — your secretary, actually
- "Build me Gmail filters so my inbox auto-sorts." *(in testing: one inbox went 50,000 unread → 264.)*
- "Categorize my inbox into Doctors / School / Bills / Fun."
- "Find newsletters I never open and draft me unsubscribe filters."
- "Summarize everything important since Friday."
- "Find appointments hidden in my email."
- "Write me a script that auto-deletes old promos but keeps anything important."

## 📅 Calendar wrangling
- "Here are 12 appointments and deadlines — organize them sensibly."
- "Find recurring things I keep forgetting."
- "What are my busiest weeks this month?"
- "Generate a calendar import file for all of this."

## 🧩 Brain-to-task translation *(THE big one)*
- "I have to do taxes and I hate it. Break it into the smallest, stupidest steps imaginable."
  > ☐ find login ☐ find W2 ☐ open software ☐ import ☐ **snack break** ☐ continue
- That's not cheating. **That's executive scaffolding.**
- "Give me 3 options and just pick one for me." (decision-paralysis breaker)
- "Body-double with me — check in every 15 minutes while I do this."

## 📂 File organization *(blows minds)*
- "Here's a folder of 700 PDFs — rename them consistently / organize by date / extract titles / deduplicate / build an index / summarize them."

## 📞 Phone-call scripts *(such an ND thing)*
- "Write me the script for this call: 'Hi, I'm calling because…' — and if they ask X, what do I say?"
- People go from *zero* dreaded calls a month to actually getting things done, because the scary uncertainty vanishes.

## 🩺 Medical chaos
- "Summarize my last three appointments." · "Make a one-page medical history."
- "List questions for my doctor." · "Compare these medications." · "Extract trends from my labs."
- "Make me an emergency info sheet." · "Bundle my symptoms into a doctor-ready summary."
- *(Info, not medical advice — but it turns chaos into something you can hand a professional.)*

## 🧹 The hidden chores *(you don't have to do these by hand)*
- Grocery lists from recipes · meal plans · price comparisons · household inventories · packing lists · vacation itineraries · subscription audits.

## 🧾 Bureaucracy translation *(so underrated)*
- "What the hell does this insurance letter mean? Explain it like I'm smart but exhausted — and tell me what I actually have to DO."
- Walk-me-through-this-form, field by field.

## 💸 Budget weirdness *(not financial advice)*
- Categorize my spending · find recurring charges & forgotten subscriptions · estimate upcoming bills · predict payday cash flow.

## 🤖 Computer stuff *(wildly capable)*
- Batch-rename files · convert PDFs · resize 400 images · build a spreadsheet · write a script · automate backups · generate an HTML page · make import/export files (like those Gmail filters).

## 🛑 Hyperfocus guardrails *(a personal favorite)*
- "I've been researching this for three hours. Honestly — have I already proven the case?"
  > *Yes. Put down the shovel.* 🤣
- "If I've gone past useful and the next step is really someone else's, tell me to stop."

## 🫂 Social scaffolding
- "Remind me who I haven't talked to lately."
- "Draft a low-pressure message to a friend."
- "I want to reconnect but don't know what to say."
- "Is this message too harsh / too apologetic before I send it?"

## 🌱 Human stuff *(this one's tender)*
- "I have no hobbies anymore. What did I used to love?"
- "I forgot what I do for joy — help me remember."
- Sometimes another brain helps you find your way back to yourself.

---

## So, the actual list:

**You are allowed to ask an AI to hold:**

> memory · organization · scripts · summaries · delegation · gentle interruptions · stopping points · emotional scaffolding ·

…and sometimes, a tiny octopus that says:

> **Hey goblin. You ate breakfast. That counts.** 🐙💜

A lot of people genuinely don't know that's possible yet. Now you do. Talk to your Claude. Take what helps. Leave the rest.
